# The Big Ball of Mud

The design/implementation of the Trading Post system on this branch is an example of a typical "big ball of mud" that
comes to mind when we think of a "monolith". It is important to note that being a ball of mud has nothing to do with it
being a monolith. Microservices can be their own little balls of mud and a well architected monolith is far from being a
ball of mud.
